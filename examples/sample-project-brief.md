# Mechanical Package - Project Brief (sample for RAG demo)

## Background

The mechanical package covers all rotating and static equipment for the LNG
train. Vendor A is responsible for compressor skid drawings while vendor B
supplies the cryogenic pumps.

## Schedule impact

In August the mechanical package experienced a 21 day approval delay caused
by late submission of vendor A drawings. Recovery plan requires weekend shifts
in the engineering team.

## Safety rules

All cryogenic areas require two person verification before any valve operation.
Hydrotest boundaries must be locked and tagged before pressurisation begins.

## Commercial terms

Liquidated damages apply at 0.5 percent per week of contractor delay.
Payment milestones follow the FIDIC Silver Book schedule.
